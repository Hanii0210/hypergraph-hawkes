# Synthetic design

## Seeds and repetitions

The master seed is 20260929. `seed_for` in
`experiments/synthetic_pipeline.py` applies NumPy `SeedSequence` to
`[master_seed, experiment_id, scenario_id, phase_id, repeat, purpose, model, start]`
and produces one uint64 seed. Phase IDs are 1 for pilot and 2 for formal.
Scenario, purpose, and model IDs are explicit in the source. Generation and
the three optimization starts use separately recorded seeds.

Syn13 has six settings with 100 paths each. Syn14 adds four periodic settings
with 100 paths each and two linear settings with 50 paths each. Its
constant-background controls reuse 200 Syn13 paths and add two
varying-background fits per path. The formal totals are 1100 distinct paths,
4200 fits, and 12600 starts. Pilot repetitions, including the doubled burn-in
check, are excluded from these totals.

## Estimation and prediction

All study fits use source-allocation EM followed by coordinate maximization.
The fixed configuration specifies the stopping tolerances and iteration
budgets. The selected fit maximizes the training objective among qualified
starts. Three-node prediction compares C-M2 with C-M1, or V-M2 with V-M1,
on the same path. Test scores are complete unpenalized conditional log
likelihoods. Monte Carlo standard errors use independent paths.

The saved formal records cover all 1100 datasets and 4200 qualified fits.
All 12600 final starts qualified after the complete EM-plus-coordinate
procedure; this does not mean that every EM stage qualified before refinement.

## Saved records and new runs

Publication records are in `records/synthetic`, with formal study metadata in
`records/synthetic/study/formal`. Individual start summaries are in
`records/derived/synthetic_starts.jsonl`. See [record schema](run_records.md).
Synthetic event arrays are regenerated by the study runner rather than stored
in this compact publication archive.

A new run writes to `experiments/results/synthetic`. Each dataset working
folder contains `dataset.json`, `events.npz`, `comparisons.json`, model
`fit.json` files, and three full start records per model. The formal aggregate
is `synthetic_experiments/formal/summary.json` below that result root.
Event-cap retries and unresolved statuses remain in the working records.
Inspect the scientific counts and qualification fields rather than relying
on a top-level invocation status.
